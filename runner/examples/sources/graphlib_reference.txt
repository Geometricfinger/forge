Excerpt: "Marks a set of nodes returned by get_ready as processed, unblocking any successor"
Contributor paraphrase: A topological ordering respects predecessor dependencies. A complete ordering requires an acyclic directed graph. Preparation can expose a cycle. Ready nodes and completed nodes are distinct states. Completion is reported with done; fetching a ready node is not equivalent to completing its work.
